REFUND AND CANCELLATION - PROPOSED INSTITUTE REVIEW DRAFT

These are proposed terms requiring institute approval. They are not a published refund promise. Obtain the final written policy and course offer before payment.

Proposed eligibility
Before the first scheduled session and before paid recording/material access begins, a learner may request a full refund of the course fee paid. After delivery or paid access begins, assess the unused portion fairly against the agreed service and give a written calculation; do not apply a blanket no-refund rule. The institute must approve the calculation method before publishing this policy.

Institute cancellation or material non-delivery
Where the institute cancels an undelivered course, offer a full refund or a mutually agreed transfer. Review deficient service, material differences from the written offer, prolonged interruption and statutory remedies separately. A learner should not be forced into a transfer instead of an available refund.

Duplicate or excess payments
Verify the payment reference and refund the excess through the appropriate original payment channel where possible. Do not ask for a card security code, UPI PIN or password.

Request process
Email xpertsedgetechlms@gmail.com with name, course/batch, payment date/reference, amount and cancellation reason. Call +91 88707 83300 for assistance. Use a private channel for supporting evidence; do not publish bank details.

Proposed service targets
Acknowledge within 2 working days. After approval, aim to initiate the refund within 7 working days. Bank/provider settlement time may vary. These targets require finance approval. Any valid deduction must be disclosed and explained in advance; do not invent new deductions after payment.

Review and statutory rights
Give the learner a written decision, calculation and escalation contact. Nothing in this draft excludes remedies required by applicable law. Before publication, confirm the legal entity/address, refund decision-maker, delivery/access calculation, transfers, taxes/payment charges, timelines and grievance arrangements. No final effective date is assigned until approval.
