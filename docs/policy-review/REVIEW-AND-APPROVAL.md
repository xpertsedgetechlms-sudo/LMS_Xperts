# Institute review and approval

Status: NOT APPROVED. Prepared 23 October 2026. The assistant has not signed, approved or published these policies. An authenticated admin action records the institute's attestation; it does not establish legal compliance by itself.

The three accompanying drafts replace the obsolete browser-only wording with the current Spring Boot/PostgreSQL model. They are proposals, not legal advice or a guarantee that all obligations are satisfied.

## Confirm before approval

1. Institute legal entity, registration details, physical address and the authorised policy owner.
2. Exact syllabus/fees/taxes, delivery and access duration, support promises, complaints/escalation contacts and learner eligibility.
3. Refund calculation for used/unused training and accessed content; cancellations/transfers, deductions, duplicate payments and finance-approved response/refund targets. The proposed 2-working-day acknowledgement and 7-working-day initiation targets are not yet institute commitments.
4. Hosting location, actual processors (hosting, SMTP, meeting/WhatsApp and future payments), data inventory including browser-held modules, lawful processing arrangements and any notices/choices required.
5. Actual retention/deletion/backup schedule, request-handling procedure, security incident response and who handles privacy grievances.
6. Age eligibility and guardian process for minors; do not assume this is implemented.
7. Verify applicable Indian consumer/data-protection requirements and the relevant commencement dates with a qualified reviewer. Do not advertise guaranteed placement, unsupported accreditation or blanket exclusions of statutory remedies.
8. Certificate wording and issuer authority. The new PDF is an institute completion record, not a digitally signed certificate or accreditation claim. No signature has been fabricated.

## Product workflow

Restart the backend to apply V10. Admin -> Settings -> Institute policy review. Select each policy, edit the wording and Save review draft. Drafts remain private; an earlier approved version stays public until replaced. After the authorised person finishes review, remove draft labels and unresolved review notes, save, tick the approval attestation, then Approve and publish policy. The server records that admin's identity, approval time and revision. Conflicting edits require a refresh.

Public Privacy/Terms/Refund pages show only a published version. Until approval they explicitly say no approved policy is available and direct learners to contact the institute before payment. The app does not automatically obtain learner consent or prove acceptance of a version; decide and implement any required enrolment acceptance/audit process before paid rollout.

Archive the approved text, reviewer/role, version, approval date, effective date and subsequent notices in the institute's records. Have the actual policy owner sign off; no signature or approval is supplied by this document.
