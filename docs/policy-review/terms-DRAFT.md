Accounts and acceptable use
Provide accurate information, protect your password and do not share accounts or attempt to bypass role/course access controls. Report suspected misuse promptly. Do not harass others, upload unlawful material, interfere with services or use another learner's work as your own.

Course offer
The written offer must confirm syllabus, fee and taxes, delivery mode, batch dates, trainer/support arrangements, access period, prerequisites, assessment and certificate requirements. Schedule or trainer changes must be communicated, with fair options where a material change affects the agreed service.

Learning materials and assessments
Course materials remain subject to institute/licensor rights. Use them for your own learning; do not redistribute recordings, private class links or another learner's personal information. Submit your own work and follow the stated assessment rules. Suspected misconduct should be reviewed fairly with an opportunity to respond.

Certificates
A completion certificate records the institute's assessment of course requirements. It is not a degree, professional licence, job guarantee or accreditation claim unless separately supported in writing. Certificates may be corrected or revoked for substantiated error or misconduct; explain the reason and review channel.

Maintenance and third-party outages may interrupt access. The institute should communicate material interruptions and arrange reasonable learning support or remedies. Nothing here excludes liability or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. Retain your original assignment files and payment references.
