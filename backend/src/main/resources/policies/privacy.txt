PRIVACY NOTICE - INSTITUTE REVIEW DRAFT

XpertsEdge Technologies uses personal information to provide and administer training. This draft is not an approved public policy.

Information used
We process account name, email, password hash, optional profile/contact information, enrolments, attendance, learning progress, quiz answers/results, assignments and uploaded work, certificates, fee/receipt records, support requests and activity/delivery records. Do not upload government identity documents or sensitive information unless the institute has separately explained the need and arranged a suitable process.

Purpose and access
Records support account access, course administration, teaching, assessment, certificates, fee reconciliation, security and service support. Administrators and assigned trainers have role-based access. We do not use this notice to claim that employment or assessment outcomes are guaranteed.

Storage and service providers
Core records and uploaded learning files are stored in PostgreSQL on the institute's configured infrastructure. Browser cookies maintain sign-in sessions; local preferences or cached workspace records may also remain on the device. Use a trusted device and sign out after use. Hosting, SMTP email, meeting, WhatsApp and any future payment provider may process information when their services are enabled. The institute must identify the actual providers and hosting locations before approval. Payment PINs and card security codes must never be sent to institute support.

Communication
Course-access, assignment and class notices may be sent to the registered email. Password-reset links expire and can be used once. External meeting and WhatsApp services apply their own terms. Marketing or testimonials require a separate appropriate choice; this notice does not authorise them.

Retention and requests
Keep information only for training, security, accounting or applicable legal needs. The institute must approve a retention/deletion schedule, including backups and browser-held records, before publication. Learners may request access, correction or deletion through xpertsedgetechlms@gmail.com or +91 88707 83300. Some records may need to be retained where required; explain the reason and available remedies. These requests currently require institute support and are not an automatic account-delete feature.

Learners under 18
The institute must confirm its age/guardian-consent process and applicable requirements before accepting minors. This draft does not represent that such a process is already implemented.

Security and complaints
Use access controls, private backups and incident-response procedures. No system can promise absolute security. Contact the institute for privacy concerns; the authorised reviewer must confirm grievance contact details, response arrangements, applicable legal requirements and commencement dates. No effective date applies until this notice is approved and published.
